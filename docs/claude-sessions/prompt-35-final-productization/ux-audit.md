# UX Audit & B2G Design Language Evaluation

## 1. Visual Hierarchy & Government-Grade Design Language

TourSafe avoids consumer app trends and adheres to a mission-critical, authoritative B2G aesthetic:
- **Calm, High-Contrast Palette**: Deep slate `#0B132B` and `#0F172A` backgrounds reduce eye fatigue during 24/7 emergency monitoring shifts.
- **Strict Semantic Color Rules**:
  - `Red (#C53030)`: Reserved strictly for Critical SOS emergencies, confirmed hazard breaches, and system load-shedding states.
  - `Amber (#FF6B00 / #F59E0B)`: Warning states, elevated risk, and pending human verifications.
  - `Emerald (#046A38 / #10B981)`: Operational safety, verified KYC, and normal baseline health.
  - `Teal (#0D7680 / #2DD4BF)`: Sovereign DPDP compliance, secondary actions, and AI grounding citations.
- **High-Density Typography**: Sans-serif system fonts with explicit uppercase section trackers and monospace coordinate readouts for tactical accuracy.

---

## 2. Interaction Flows & Ergonomics

### Tourist Safety Companion:
- **1-Touch SOS Button**: Located prominently with a 3-second physical countdown and haptic feedback to eliminate false positives while maintaining instantaneous emergency access.
- **TSQR Credential Pass**: Single tap opens full-screen high-contrast QR pass for rapid scanning at forest checkposts and tourist gates.

### Authority Command Center:
- **Triage Speed**: Single-click triage actions for incident dispatch, responder assignment, and status updates directly from the live map popup or triage queue table.
- **AI Grounding Clarity**: Copilot answers display explicit source citation badges and require separate operator confirmation before executing any system mutations.

### Field Responder Operations:
- **High-Glanceability Layout**: Large touch targets for gloved operation, high-contrast status toggle switches (`EN_ROUTE`, `ON_SCENE`, `RESOLVED`), and instant offline sync indicators.
