## 2. Key Privacy & Governance Capabilities

1. **Unbundled Sovereign Consent Engine**:
   - Explicit, granular opt-ins for Location Tracking, IMU Kinematics, and KYC Credentialing.
   - Immediate cryptographic recording of consent withdrawals with timestamped audit trails.
2. **Data Subject Requests (DSR) & Portability**:
   - Automated 72-hour statutory deadline countdown for Access, Correction, and Deletion requests.
   - Machine-readable JSON data export bundle secured with 24-hour signed download tokens.
3. **Automated Data Retention & Legal Holds**:
   - Dynamic retention sweeps (e.g. 90 days for location telemetry, 365 days for verified KYC).
   - Protective legal hold placement (`createLegalHold`) by authorized investigators, instantly halting retention sweeps on active case records.
4. **Privileged Access Management (PAM) & Break-Glass**:
   - Time-bounded (2-hour) emergency elevation with mandatory justification notes and immutable hash-chained audit logs.
